# v0.24 — Floor Select UX

- Replaced scrolling floor list with World tabs and paired floor buttons.
- Visually grouped floors 1–8 as Stone, 9–10 as Magma per world, without inventing new ore types or floor rewards. These are UI groupings rather than verified reference world progression formulas.
- Worlds are ten floors each, with later worlds disabled until an accessible floor exists within them.
- Shows current floor in green, accessible floors in red, and locked floors in charcoal.
- Preserves existing floor progress, manual travel, Auto-Floor and saved data.
- Dedicated lower-center close control; mining background is hidden while Floor Select is open.
- No art or balance changes.
