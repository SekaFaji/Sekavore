# Sekavore v0.33 - Workshop purchases

- Functional Basic & Chain Damage Workshop upgrade at Obelisk 1, maximum level 22, +0.5x per level to Basic Bomb damage. A future Chain Bomb can use the same modifier.
- Crafting materials are Mithril, Adamant and Runite bars. These resource types and level caps are documented, but the exact quantities are NOT confirmed. Prototype costs are (current level + 1) of each bar.
- Costs and owned amounts shown on the Workshop button. BUY / NEED BARS / OBELISK / MAXED states are live.
- Purchases are atomic, permanent, and preserved across Prestige and older saves.
- Unsupported bomb blueprints show documented bar types and gates but remain disabled.
- Artwork and unrelated systems unchanged. New deployment assets are included and verified.
