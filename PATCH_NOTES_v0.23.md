# Sekavore v0.23 — Main controls

- Upper field shortcut now opens Obelisk Fight instead of the Drone management menu.
- New dedicated top-right Stats and Currencies controls.
- Stats has three data-backed pages (Pickaxe/Bombs/Drones/Misc; Progress/Economy; Activity).
- Currencies has compact ore/bar and funds tables, with Ore Bag still accessible independently.
- Save format, mining economy, progression, Bombs and Consumables are unchanged.
- No new artwork or gameplay content.
