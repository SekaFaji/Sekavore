# Sekavore v0.35 - Floor control and mined-node visibility

- Combined up/down button opens Floor Select and its floor/ore overview.
- Up arrow moves to the next floor when unlocked. Large lower control shows the remaining full-wave clears on the deepest floor; on an earlier unlocked floor it becomes a down arrow and returns toward the deepest reached floor.
- Mined nodes stay visible but darkened, untargetable and unrewardable until all nodes are destroyed; one full wave counts as one floor clear. The existing provisional 150-wave quota and 1.25s respawn timing are unchanged.
- Bombs, drone, hand, auto-bomber and selected-node HUD ignore darkened nodes.
- Save format and other economy systems unchanged. Existing floor progress is retained.
