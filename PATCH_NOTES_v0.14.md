# Sekavore v0.14 — Drone Targeting and Animation

- Replaced random remote drone attacks with nearest-rock target acquisition.
- Drone sprite travels to its selected target before damage is applied.
- Cyan impact flash marks the rock actually hit; no damage is applied to a different location.
- Rock removal or floor transition invalidates the old target safely.
- Maintains the existing drone purchase/upgrade formulas, 250 ms cooldown tick, and local save format.
- Bombs, Consumables, and drone management remain separate. No new gameplay content.
