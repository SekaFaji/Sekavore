# Sekavore v0.36 - Floor direction and Auto Next correction

- ▲ now travels to the preceding floor number, e.g. 5 to 4; at floor 1 it cannot travel further up.
- The lower contextual button now travels downward to the next higher-numbered floor, e.g. 5 to 6, once unlocked. Until then it shows full clears remaining; when unlocked it shows ▼.
- AUTO NEXT is grey when off (or locked) and green when active; exposes pressed state for assistive technology.
- When active and the floor reaches its full-clear target, Auto Next advances exactly one floor immediately. Turning it on after already meeting the requirement also advances once.
- The Skill Tree unlock gate for Auto Next, existing floor quotas, saves, progression and mining behavior are unchanged.
