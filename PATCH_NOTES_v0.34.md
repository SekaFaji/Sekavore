# Sekavore v0.34 - Special bombs

- Workshop unlocks Bomb of Plenty (Obelisk 1; Copper/Mithril/Adamant) and Experience Bomb (Obelisk 2; Adamant/Runite/Citrine).
- Exact bar counts are unverified: prototype quantity is one of each required bar. Capacity 3 and recharge 9 seconds are also provisional.
- Unlocked-only bomb slots appear with independent charges.
- Bomb of Plenty marks current rocks for 2x ore when mined. Experience Bomb marks current rocks for 2x mining XP when mined. Marks can stack; they do not directly damage rocks or carry across respawn waves.
- Basic Bomb, Auto-Bomber, save key and art unchanged. Workshop purchases and special bomb charge state survive Prestige.
